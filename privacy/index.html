Privacy

Voicegames is an independent browser game site at voicegames.org. There is no account.

Microphone

The microphone stays off until you press Start. Press Stop, or let the round end, and the page releases it.

Say it, Count, Repeat, Voice over, and Voice acting use the speech recognition built into desktop Chrome. That sends audio to Chrome’s speech service so the page can read the words. Voicegames does not receive that audio, does not keep a transcript, and does not sell it. The words are used only to score the round on your screen. On Voice acting, the mood is shown for you to play in. The score uses the line, not the mood.

Louder, Dino, Flap, Float, and the 3D block games measure volume in the page. The audio stays on your device. It is not sent to a speech service, and no recording is saved.

What this site stores

Voicegames does not set cookies and does not run analytics. The score and the clock live in the page until you leave or play again. Type is loaded from Google Fonts.
