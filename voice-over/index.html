How to play Voice over

  1. Press Start and allow the microphone.
  2. A short narration line fills the screen, like “the train is late”.
  3. Read every word. The score moves when the whole line lands.
  4. A new line appears each time. You have 60 seconds.

The lines are written for this game. Voice Over uses Chrome’s speech service to turn your voice into text. This page does not store the recording. Open it in desktop Chrome.

Questions about Voice over

Is Voice over free?

Yes. Every game on Voicegames is free, with no account and no download. Open the page and play.

Where does Voice over work?

The speaking games need a microphone and desktop Chrome, on a computer. Chrome sends your audio to its speech service and shows the words on the page.

Is my voice recorded?

No. The volume games measure loudness inside the page and never keep audio. The speaking games use Chrome’s built-in speech service to turn speech into text on your screen; Voicegames never receives or stores the audio.

Is Voice Over good for reading practice?

That is the idea. Lines are short and natural, and the game only hears whether the words landed — so it doubles as a read-aloud drill.

How is this different from the Voice over game page?

This one is our own 60-second word game that runs here. The other page embeds an external studio, Choicer Voicer, for full dubbing projects.
