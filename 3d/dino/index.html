How to play Dino 3D

  1. Press Start, then allow the microphone if the browser asks.
  2. Hold a sound, the Space bar, or the mouse button and the dinosaur stays up. Let go and it falls.
  3. Each stone you clear adds one point.
  4. Touch a stone and the round ends. You have 30 seconds.

You see the dinosaur from the side. Stones come in from the right. Dino 3D measures volume in the page. It does not send audio to a speech service and it does not keep a recording.

Questions about Dino 3D

Is Dino 3D free?

Yes. Every game on Voicegames is free, with no account and no download. Open the page and play.

Where does Dino 3D work?

It runs in any modern browser, on desktop and mobile. Space and the mouse also control the game, so it works even if the microphone is blocked.

Is my voice recorded?

No. The volume games measure loudness inside the page and never keep audio. The speaking games use Chrome’s built-in speech service to turn speech into text on your screen; Voicegames never receives or stores the audio.

How do I score in Dino 3D?

Every stone the dinosaur clears adds one point before the 30 seconds run out.

Do I have to shout?

No. Any steady sound holds the dinosaur up — talking, humming, or holding Space works too. Shouting is just funnier.
