How to play Dino 3D

Press Start. Hold a sound, the Space bar, or the mouse button on the picture and the dinosaur stays up. Let go and it falls. Each stone you clear adds one. Touch a stone and the round ends. You have 30 seconds. Space also starts the round. The picture still works if the microphone is blocked.

You see the dinosaur from the side. Stones come in from the right. Dino 3D measures volume in the page. It does not send audio to a speech service and it does not keep a recording.
