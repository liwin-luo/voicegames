How to play Float 3D

Press Start and allow the microphone. A balloon is ahead of you. A louder sound lifts it. Quiet lets it sink. Rings come toward you along the track. Pass through a ring to score. Touch the ring and the round ends. You have 30 seconds.

You look down the track from behind the balloon. Each hole is a ring standing on the path, and the score still uses a circle. Float 3D measures volume in the page. It does not send audio to a speech service and it does not keep a recording.
