How to play Float 3D

  1. Press Start, then allow the microphone if the browser asks.
  2. A louder sound lifts the balloon; hold Space to rise, or hold the mouse and move up and down.
  3. Pass through each round hole to score a point.
  4. Touch a hole and the round ends. You have 30 seconds.

You see the balloon from the side, and the holes are rings that slide in from the right. Float 3D measures volume in the page. It does not send audio to a speech service and it does not keep a recording.

Questions about Float 3D

Is Float 3D free?

Yes. Every game on Voicegames is free, with no account and no download. Open the page and play.

Where does Float 3D work?

It runs in any modern browser, on desktop and mobile. Space and the mouse also control the game, so it works even if the microphone is blocked.

Is my voice recorded?

No. The volume games measure loudness inside the page and never keep audio. The speaking games use Chrome’s built-in speech service to turn speech into text on your screen; Voicegames never receives or stores the audio.

How is Float 3D different from the other 3D games?

Volume is a height dial, not just an on/off lift: the louder you are, the higher the balloon sits. Fine control wins.

Can I play with the mouse only?

Yes. Hold the mouse button on the picture and move up or down — the balloon follows your pointer. Space also works.
