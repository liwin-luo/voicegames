How to play Float 3D

Press Start. A louder sound lifts the balloon. Hold Space to rise, or hold the mouse on the picture and move up and down. Quiet, and releasing the key, lets it sink. Pass through a ring to score. Touch the ring and the round ends. You have 30 seconds. Space also starts the round. The picture still works if the microphone is blocked.

You see the balloon from the side. Each hole is a ring, and the score still uses a circle. Float 3D measures volume in the page. It does not send audio to a speech service and it does not keep a recording.
