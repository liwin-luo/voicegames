How to play Flap 3D

  1. Press Start, then allow the microphone if the browser asks.
  2. Hold a sound, the Space bar, or the mouse button and the moth rises. Let go and it falls.
  3. Fly through a gap to score a point.
  4. Touch a wall, the top, or the ground and the round ends. You have 30 seconds.

You see the moth from the side. The gaps come in from the right. Flap 3D measures volume in the page. It does not send audio to a speech service and it does not keep a recording.

Questions about Flap 3D

Is Flap 3D free?

Yes. Every game on Voicegames is free, with no account and no download. Open the page and play.

Where does Flap 3D work?

It runs in any modern browser, on desktop and mobile. Space and the mouse also control the game, so it works even if the microphone is blocked.

Is my voice recorded?

No. The volume games measure loudness inside the page and never keep audio. The speaking games use Chrome’s built-in speech service to turn speech into text on your screen; Voicegames never receives or stores the audio.

How do I score in Flap 3D?

Fly through one gap to score one point. Chain as many gaps as you can in 30 seconds.

Why does my moth keep falling?

Sound is lift. If the room is quiet the moth sinks — hold a steady sound or hold Space and it climbs.
