How to play Flap 3D

Press Start. Hold a sound, the Space bar, or the mouse button on the picture and the moth rises. Let go and it falls. Pass through a gap to score. Touch a wall, the top, or the ground and the round ends. You have 30 seconds. Space also starts the round. The picture still works if the microphone is blocked.

You see the moth from the side. The gaps come in from the right. Flap 3D measures volume in the page. It does not send audio to a speech service and it does not keep a recording.
