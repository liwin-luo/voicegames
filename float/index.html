Louder lifts the balloon. Quiet lets it fall.

How to play Float

  1. Press Start and allow the microphone.
  2. A louder sound lifts the balloon; quiet lets it sink.
  3. Pass through each round hole to score a point.
  4. Touch the wall and the round ends. You have 30 seconds.

Float measures volume in the page. It does not send audio to a speech service and it does not keep a recording. The balloon and the holes are drawn here. They are not taken from another game.

Questions about Float

Is Float free?

Yes. Every game on Voicegames is free, with no account and no download. Open the page and play.

Where does Float work?

It runs in any modern browser, on desktop and mobile. Space and the mouse also control the game, so it works even if the microphone is blocked.

Is my voice recorded?

No. The volume games measure loudness inside the page and never keep audio. The speaking games use Chrome’s built-in speech service to turn speech into text on your screen; Voicegames never receives or stores the audio.

Is Float the same as the helicopter game?

It belongs to the same one-button family, but your voice is the button and every sprite here is drawn from scratch for this site.

Why does the balloon drop so fast when I stop?

Gravity in Float is stronger than lift, on purpose — the safe line is to keep a low steady sound rather than full silence.
