The games

Dino 3D, Flap 3D, and Float 3D are the main rounds. Each one lasts 30 seconds. Sound, the Space bar, and the mouse all work, and the picture still runs if the microphone is blocked. The speaking games need a microphone and desktop Chrome. Nothing is stored on this site.

Say it shows one word at a time. Say it and the next word appears. The round lasts 60 seconds.

Count starts at one. Say each number as it appears, up to thirty, or until the minute ends.

Repeat shows a two-word line. Say both words and the next line appears.

Louder scores the seconds your voice stays loud enough. Quiet time does not count. The round lasts 30 seconds.

Dino is a picture. Hold a sound and the dinosaur stays up. Go quiet and it falls. A hit ends the round. You have 30 seconds.

Flap is a picture. Hold a sound and a pixel moth rises through the gaps. Go quiet and it falls. A hit ends the round. You have 30 seconds.

Float is a picture. Volume lifts a balloon. Quiet lets it fall. Pass through each round hole. A hit ends the round. You have 30 seconds.

Dino 3D, Flap 3D, and Float 3D use those same rules, seen from the side. Hold a sound, Space, or the mouse. Stones, gaps, and rings come in from the right.

Voice over shows a short narration line. Read the whole line and the next one appears. The round lasts 60 seconds.

Voice over game embeds Choicer Voicer. The how-to is on that page. Play opens their studio.

Voice acting shows a mood and an original line. Say the line. The mood is there to play in, and the score counts the words only. The round lasts 60 seconds.

Microphone

The microphone starts only after you press Start on a game page. Say it, Count, Repeat, Voice over, and Voice acting send audio to Chrome’s speech service to turn speech into text. Louder, Dino, Flap, Float, and the 3D block games measure volume in the page and do not keep a recording. Details are on the privacy page.
