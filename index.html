The games

Dino 3D, Flap 3D, and Float 3D are the main rounds. Each one lasts 30 seconds. Sound, the Space bar, and the mouse all work, and the picture still runs if the microphone is blocked. The speaking games need a microphone and desktop Chrome. Nothing is stored on this site.

Dino 3D Dino 3D is a free 30-second voice game. Hold a sound and a block dinosaur stays up. Go quiet and it falls. Volume stays on your device.

Flap 3D Flap 3D is a free 30-second voice game. Hold a sound and a block moth rises through the gaps. Go quiet and it falls. Volume stays on your device.

Float 3D Float 3D is a free 30-second voice game. Volume lifts a block balloon through round holes. Quiet lets it fall. Volume stays on your device.

Dino Dino is a free 30-second voice game. Hold a sound and a small dinosaur stays up. Go quiet and it falls. Volume stays on your device.

Flap Flap is a free 30-second voice game. Hold a sound and a pixel moth rises through the gaps. Go quiet and it falls. Volume stays on your device.

Float Float is a free 30-second voice game. Volume lifts a balloon through round holes. Quiet lets it fall. Volume stays on your device.

Louder Louder is a free 30-second voice game. Every second above the volume line scores. Quiet seconds do not count. Nothing is recorded.

Miner Miner is a free 30-second voice game. A claw swings over a cave — shout, press Space, or click to drop it and haul up gold. Nothing is recorded.

Floors Floors is a free 30-second voice game. A climber sinks down a shaft — hum low to walk left, sing high to walk right, and dodge the spikes. Nothing is recorded.

Catch Catch is a free 30-second voice game. Hum low or high to slide the basket, catch coins and gems, and dodge the bombs. Nothing is recorded.

Racer Racer is a free 30-second voice game. Hum low or high to steer down the road and dodge the oncoming cars. Nothing is recorded.

Bricks Bricks is a free 30-second voice game. Hum to slide the paddle, shout or press Space to launch the ball, and break as many bricks as you can. Nothing is recorded.

Snake Snake is a free 30-second voice game. A low note turns left, a high note turns right — eat apples, grow long, and don't bite yourself. Nothing is recorded.

March March is a free 30-second voice game. Hold a sound to walk down the corridor, go silent to stop — brake before the spikes, because the spike wall chases you. Nothing is recorded.

Tug Tug is a free 30-second voice game. Your volume pulls the rope against a machine that drags back harder every round. Win as many pulls as you can. Nothing is recorded.

Puff Puff is a free 30-second voice game. Hold a sound to blow the balloon up — every balloon hides its own pop size, so bank it by going quiet before it bursts. Nothing is recorded.

Hop Hop is a free 30-second voice game. Hold a sound to charge your leap, go quiet to jump — land on the next pillar and keep the charge under control. Nothing is recorded.

Miner 3D Miner 3D is a free 30-second voice game. A blocky claw swings over a blocky cave — shout, press Space, or click to drop it and haul up the gold. Nothing is recorded.

Floors 3D Floors 3D is a free 30-second voice game. A blocky climber sinks down a blocky shaft — hum low to walk left, sing high to walk right, and dodge the spikes. Nothing is recorded.

Say it Say It is a free one-minute voice game. A word appears, you say it, and the next word shows up. Play in desktop Chrome with a microphone.

Count Count is a free voice game. Say one, then two, and keep going up to thirty. You have 60 seconds. Play in desktop Chrome.

Repeat Repeat is a free voice game. A two-word line appears and you say both words. The next line shows up when the whole line lands. 60 seconds, desktop Chrome.

Voice over Voice Over is a free 60-second voice game. A short narration line appears, read it out loud, and the next one appears. Desktop Chrome with a microphone.

Voice acting Voice Acting is a free 60-second game. A mood appears above an original line — say the line and play the mood. Desktop Chrome with a microphone.

Microphone

The microphone starts only after you press Start on a game page. Say it, Count, Repeat, Voice over, and Voice acting send audio to Chrome’s speech service to turn speech into text. Every non-speaking game on the site measures volume in the page and does not keep a recording. Details are on the Privacy page.
