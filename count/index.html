How to play Count

  1. Press Start. The first number is one.
  2. Say the number on screen, then say the next one when it appears.
  3. The score adds one for every number it hears in order.
  4. The count stops at thirty, or when 60 seconds run out.

Count uses Chrome’s speech service to turn your voice into text. This page does not store the recording. Open it in desktop Chrome.

Questions about Count

Is Count free?

Yes. Every game on Voicegames is free, with no account and no download. Open the page and play.

Where does Count work?

The speaking games need a microphone and desktop Chrome, on a computer. Chrome sends your audio to its speech service and shows the words on the page.

Is my voice recorded?

No. The volume games measure loudness inside the page and never keep audio. The speaking games use Chrome’s built-in speech service to turn speech into text on your screen; Voicegames never receives or stores the audio.

What if I say a number out of order?

Nothing bad — the screen keeps asking for the same number until it hears the right one. Only correct order scores.

Does “twenty-one” have to be exact?

Close is fine. The game accepts common variants and digits — “twenty one”, “21”, regional readings — so natural counting just works.
