How to play Count

Press Start. The first number is one. Say it, then say the next number when it appears. The count stops at thirty, or when 60 seconds run out, whichever comes first.

Count uses Chrome’s speech service to turn your voice into text. This page does not store the recording. Open it in desktop Chrome.
