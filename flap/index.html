How to play Flap

  1. Press Start and allow the microphone.
  2. Hold a sound and the moth rises. Go quiet and it falls.
  3. Fly through a brick gap to score a point.
  4. Touch a wall, the top, or the ground and the round ends. You have 30 seconds.

Flap measures volume in the page. It does not send audio to a speech service and it does not keep a recording. The moth and the gates are drawn here.

Questions about Flap

Is Flap free?

Yes. Every game on Voicegames is free, with no account and no download. Open the page and play.

Where does Flap work?

It runs in any modern browser, on desktop and mobile. Space and the mouse also control the game, so it works even if the microphone is blocked.

Is my voice recorded?

No. The volume games measure loudness inside the page and never keep audio. The speaking games use Chrome’s built-in speech service to turn speech into text on your screen; Voicegames never receives or stores the audio.

What is the difference between Flap and Flap 3D?

Same rules, two pictures. Flap is the flat pixel version; Flap 3D adds a blocky side view of the same flight.

How do I keep the moth at one height?

Feed it a steady sound — a long “ahhh” at one volume holds it level. Wild volume swings make it bounce.
