Keep the sound going and the dinosaur stays up.

How to play Dino

  1. Press Start and allow the microphone.
  2. Hold a sound and the dinosaur rises, then stays up. Go quiet and it falls.
  3. Each stone you clear adds one point.
  4. Touch a stone and the round ends. You have 30 seconds.

Dino measures volume in the page. It does not send audio to a speech service and it does not keep a recording. The dinosaur and the stones are drawn here.

Questions about Dino

Is Dino free?

Yes. Every game on Voicegames is free, with no account and no download. Open the page and play.

Where does Dino work?

It runs in any modern browser, on desktop and mobile. Space and the mouse also control the game, so it works even if the microphone is blocked.

Is my voice recorded?

No. The volume games measure loudness inside the page and never keep audio. The speaking games use Chrome’s built-in speech service to turn speech into text on your screen; Voicegames never receives or stores the audio.

What is the difference between Dino and Dino 3D?

Same rules, two pictures. Dino is the flat pixel original; Dino 3D is the same run seen from a blocky side view.

Does Dino work on a phone?

Yes. It runs in a mobile browser and listens through the phone microphone. Hold the picture to play without sound.
