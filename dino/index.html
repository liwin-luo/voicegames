How to play Dino

Press Start and allow the microphone. The dinosaur runs toward stones. Hold a sound and it rises, then stays up. Go quiet and it falls. Each stone you clear adds one. Touch a stone and the round ends. You have 30 seconds.

Dino measures volume in the page. It does not send audio to a speech service and it does not keep a recording. The dinosaur and the stones are drawn here.
