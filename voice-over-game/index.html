Choicer Voicer

Voice Over Game

A voice over game is a scene you perform out loud. Choicer Voicer plays a line, you record your own take, and it builds a scene you can watch. Their studio is embedded here. The button opens their page, which is where the game runs.

Play on Choicer Voicer

What this voice over game is

Choicer Voicer is a browser studio made by someone else. You take the lines in a voice pack and say them yourself. Voicegames does not host the packs, the microphone session, or the finished scene. This page introduces the game and sends you to their studio to play.

How to play Choicer Voicer

  1. Press Play on Choicer Voicer. That leaves this page and opens their studio.
  2. Allow the microphone when their page asks.
  3. Choose a voice pack.
  4. Listen to the line, then record your take.
  5. Watch the scene they assemble from the takes.

Questions about Voice Over Game

Is Voice Over Game free?

Yes. Every game on Voicegames is free, with no account and no download. Open the page and play.

Where does Voice Over Game work?

The speaking games need a microphone and desktop Chrome, on a computer. Chrome sends your audio to its speech service and shows the words on the page.

Is my voice recorded?

No. The volume games measure loudness inside the page and never keep audio. The speaking games use Chrome’s built-in speech service to turn speech into text on your screen; Voicegames never receives or stores the audio.

Where do I play the voice over game?

On Choicer Voicer. The studio is embedded above. Play on Choicer Voicer leaves this site and opens their page.

How do I play Choicer Voicer?

Open their studio, allow the microphone, pick a voice pack, listen to the line, record your take, and watch the scene they assemble.
