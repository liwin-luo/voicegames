How to play Voice Acting

Press Start and allow the microphone. A mood appears above an original line, such as whisper or angry. Say the line. The score goes up when every word of the line is heard, then a new mood and line appear. You have 60 seconds.

The mood is for you to play in. This page cannot hear whether you sounded angry or quiet, so it does not score the performance. It only checks the words. The lines are written for this game. Voice Acting uses Chrome’s speech service to turn your voice into text. This page does not store the recording. Open it in desktop Chrome.
