How to play Voice acting

  1. Press Start and allow the microphone.
  2. A mood appears above an original line, like whisper or angry.
  3. Say the line — and play the mood while you do.
  4. The score counts the words. The mood is yours to act. You have 60 seconds.

This page cannot hear whether you sounded angry or quiet, so it does not score the performance — it only checks the words. The lines are written for this game. Voice Acting uses Chrome’s speech service to turn your voice into text, and the page does not store the recording.

Questions about Voice acting

Is Voice acting free?

Yes. Every game on Voicegames is free, with no account and no download. Open the page and play.

Where does Voice acting work?

The speaking games need a microphone and desktop Chrome, on a computer. Chrome sends your audio to its speech service and shows the words on the page.

Is my voice recorded?

No. The volume games measure loudness inside the page and never keep audio. The speaking games use Chrome’s built-in speech service to turn speech into text on your screen; Voicegames never receives or stores the audio.

Does acting the mood change my score?

No — the score only checks that the words landed. The mood is there to make it fun and to practice range.

Can I use Voice acting for auditions or demos?

It is a handy warm-up for exactly that: random moods, short lines, one minute on the clock. For real recordings use a proper studio — this page keeps no audio.
