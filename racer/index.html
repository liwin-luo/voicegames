Steer down the road and dodge the cars.

How to play Racer

  1. Press Start and allow the microphone.
  2. Hum low to steer left, high to steer right. Arrow keys and holding each half of the picture work too.
  3. Every car you pass scores one point. Touch one and the round ends.
  4. Traffic speeds up as you go. You have 30 seconds.

Racer measures pitch in the page. The audio never leaves your device and nothing is recorded. The road, the cars, and the crash are drawn here.

Questions about Racer

Is Racer free?

Yes. Every game on Voicegames is free, with no account and no download. Open the page and play.

Where does Racer work?

It runs in any modern browser, on desktop and mobile. Space and the mouse also control the game, so it works even if the microphone is blocked.

Is my voice recorded?

No. The volume games measure loudness inside the page and never keep audio. The speaking games use Chrome’s built-in speech service to turn speech into text on your screen; Voicegames never receives or stores the audio.

Does the car stay in a lane?

No — steering is continuous, like a real wheel. Small corrections are enough; big swings put you into the next lane's bumper.

How fast does it get?

Traffic adds a little pace with every car you pass, so the final ten seconds are the real test.
