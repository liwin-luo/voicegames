How to play Repeat

  1. Press Start and allow the microphone.
  2. A short line of two words appears, such as “red apple”.
  3. Say both words. The score only moves when the whole line is heard.
  4. A new line appears each time. You have 60 seconds.

Repeat uses Chrome’s speech service to turn your voice into text. This page does not store the recording. Open it in desktop Chrome.

Questions about Repeat

Is Repeat free?

Yes. Every game on Voicegames is free, with no account and no download. Open the page and play.

Where does Repeat work?

The speaking games need a microphone and desktop Chrome, on a computer. Chrome sends your audio to its speech service and shows the words on the page.

Is my voice recorded?

No. The volume games measure loudness inside the page and never keep audio. The speaking games use Chrome’s built-in speech service to turn speech into text on your screen; Voicegames never receives or stores the audio.

Do word order and extra words matter?

All words of the line must be heard; extra words around them are ignored, so natural speech is fine. Wrong order does not score.

Can kids play Repeat?

It is a popular reading warm-up: short lines, no timer pressure per line, and the score is just words heard. Try it together.
