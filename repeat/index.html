How to play Repeat

Press Start. A short line of two words appears, such as “red apple”. Say both words. The score increases only when the whole line is heard, and then a new line appears. You have 60 seconds.

Repeat uses Chrome’s speech service to turn your voice into text. This page does not store the recording. Open it in desktop Chrome.
