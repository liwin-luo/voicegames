How to play Louder

Press Start and allow the microphone. Speak, sing, or just make noise. The score adds a second only while the microphone hears you above the line. If the room goes quiet, the score pauses and the word changes to Louder. The round lasts 30 seconds.

Louder measures volume in this page. It does not send the audio to a speech service and it does not store a recording.
