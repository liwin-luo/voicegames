How to play Louder

  1. Press Start and allow the microphone.
  2. Speak, sing, or just make noise above the volume line.
  3. Every second above the line adds one to your score. Quiet time does not count.
  4. The round lasts 30 seconds. Beat your best.

Louder measures volume in this page. It does not send the audio to a speech service and it does not store a recording. The word on the screen only tells you whether the level is over the line.

Questions about Louder

Is Louder free?

Yes. Every game on Voicegames is free, with no account and no download. Open the page and play.

Where does Louder work?

It runs in any modern browser, on desktop and mobile. Space and the mouse also control the game, so it works even if the microphone is blocked.

Is my voice recorded?

No. The volume games measure loudness inside the page and never keep audio. The speaking games use Chrome’s built-in speech service to turn speech into text on your screen; Voicegames never receives or stores the audio.

What counts as “loud enough”?

Normal speaking volume clears the line in a quiet room. If the word on screen says “Louder”, the level is under it — push a little more.

Can I score with music or a video?

Technically yes — the game only hears volume. But the fun is in making the noise yourself.
