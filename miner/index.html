How to play Miner

  1. Press Start and allow the microphone.
  2. The claw swings by itself. Shout, press Space, or click and it shoots straight down.
  3. It hauls back whatever it grabs — gold pays well, rock is heavy and slow, and the blue gem is the jackpot.
  4. Grab as much as you can in 30 seconds.

Miner measures volume in the page. It does not send audio to a speech service and it does not keep a recording. The claw, the gold, and the cave are drawn here.

Questions about Miner

Is Miner free?

Yes. Every game on Voicegames is free, with no account and no download. Open the page and play.

Where does Miner work?

It runs in any modern browser, on desktop and mobile. Space and the mouse also control the game, so it works even if the microphone is blocked.

Is my voice recorded?

No. The volume games measure loudness inside the page and never keep audio. The speaking games use Chrome’s built-in speech service to turn speech into text on your screen; Voicegames never receives or stores the audio.

How do I score more in Miner?

Wait until the claw points at a nugget before you shout. Big gold pays 120 and the blue gem pays 300 — line it up first.

Do I have to shout?

No — any short sound past the volume line drops the claw, and Space or a click works with the microphone off.
