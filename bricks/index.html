How to play Bricks

  1. Press Start and allow the microphone.
  2. Hum low or high to slide the paddle; the ball rides it until you launch.
  3. Shout, press Space, or click to launch. Top gold rows pay 3, middle rows 2, bottom row 1.
  4. If the ball drops, slide in and shout to serve again. You have 30 seconds.

Bricks measures volume and pitch in the page. The audio never leaves your device and nothing is recorded. The wall, the paddle, and the ball are drawn here.

Questions about Bricks

Is Bricks free?

Yes. Every game on Voicegames is free, with no account and no download. Open the page and play.

Where does Bricks work?

It runs in any modern browser, on desktop and mobile. Space and the mouse also control the game, so it works even if the microphone is blocked.

Is my voice recorded?

No. The volume games measure loudness inside the page and never keep audio. The speaking games use Chrome’s built-in speech service to turn speech into text on your screen; Voicegames never receives or stores the audio.

Why does the ball need relaunching?

The shout is your serve. When the ball drops past the paddle, slide to a smart spot and shout again — launching well is part of the score.

Which bricks pay most?

The two top gold rows pay 3 each, the two middle rows pay 2, and the bottom row pays 1 — keep the ball high.
