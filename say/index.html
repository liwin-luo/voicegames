How to play Say It

Press Start and allow the microphone. One word fills the screen. Say that word clearly. When it matches, the score goes up and a new word appears. You have 60 seconds. Press Stop to end early, or Play again for another minute.

Say It uses Chrome’s speech service to turn your voice into text. This page does not store the recording. Open it in desktop Chrome.
