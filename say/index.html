How to play Say it

  1. Press Start and allow the microphone.
  2. One word fills the screen. Say that word clearly.
  3. When it matches, the score goes up and a new word appears.
  4. You have 60 seconds. Press Stop to end early.

Say It uses Chrome’s speech service to turn your voice into text. This page does not store the recording. Open it in desktop Chrome.

Questions about Say it

Is Say it free?

Yes. Every game on Voicegames is free, with no account and no download. Open the page and play.

Where does Say it work?

The speaking games need a microphone and desktop Chrome, on a computer. Chrome sends your audio to its speech service and shows the words on the page.

Is my voice recorded?

No. The volume games measure loudness inside the page and never keep audio. The speaking games use Chrome’s built-in speech service to turn speech into text on your screen; Voicegames never receives or stores the audio.

Why is my word not counting?

Say the whole word clearly at normal volume. The bottom strip shows what Chrome heard — compare it with the word on screen and try again.

Can I play Say It in other languages?

Yes — switch the site language in the top bar and the words change with it. Each version listens in its own language.
